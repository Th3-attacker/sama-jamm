## Texte proposé (anglais, accepté par Apple ; version française ci-dessous)
Sama Jàmm is a free app without ads: Quran reading and audio, prayer times, qibla, dhikr, household budget, collective Quran reading (khatm). The app works fully **without an account**; sign-in with Apple or Google is optional and only needed to create/join a khatm.
- Location (when in use): prayer times and qibla, computed on the device, never sent.
- Notifications and alarms: local prayer reminders and adhan.
- Face ID: optional lock of the budget, which is stored encrypted on the device only.
- Donations are optional and paid outside the app through a mobile wallet; the app only records a declaration. No in-app purchase.
- Account deletion: More › Account › Delete.
- Demo account for the khatm: **À CRÉER (email/identifiants à fournir)**. Sign in with Apple needs no account from us.
